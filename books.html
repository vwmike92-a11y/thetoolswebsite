The Tools Winter Rescue

Join Hector Hammer and his friends this winter as they search for trucks in need of roadside rescue so that the town can get their building supplies. There is always work to do for the Tools — but not without fun, laughter and complete chaos!

Meanwhile Paz the Paintbrush is causing chaos at every turn and attracts the attention of a rather large moose — what could possibly go wrong?

Just another day in Twin Falls.

Book 3

The Tools and the Railway

Patrick the Pickaxe is loading up his train at the quarry — right on time, for once. But when a family of beavers takes a liking to the wooden railway bridge, Twin Falls has a real problem on its hands.

Roxie snowboards down the mountain to help, Diesel's wagons go rolling off without their brakes, and Wayne ends up covered head to toe in paint. Somehow, the whole crew pulls together to fix the bridge — and Patrick's famous lateness turns out to be exactly what saves the day.

Book 5

The Tools Light Up Christmas

Christmas is coming to Twin Falls! The crew are decorating the whole town and building a beautiful nativity scene for Christmas Eve — but Diesel was supposed to fix the Christmas lights, and he's completely forgotten.

Add a runaway donkey, a very tangled Paz, a snowball fight that gets out of hand and a camel nobody can quite explain, and Christmas is heading for disaster. When the lights go out and Santa's sleigh comes down in the dark, it's up to the whole crew to put things right — just in time to see a bright star shining over Twin Falls.

A Christ-centred Christmas story, full of laughter, kindness and the true meaning of Christmas.
