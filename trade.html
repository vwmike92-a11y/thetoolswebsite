📧 Place an Order

How to order

Ordering takes a single email. There's no account to set up and no forms to fill in.

1

Choose your books

Pick your titles and quantities. Mix and match across the whole series — the minimum is just 10 books in total.

2

Email your order

Send your list to mike@thetools.online and I'll confirm the total, including shipping.

3

Pay the invoice

You'll receive an invoice you can pay securely by card.

4

Books arrive

Books are printed and shipped direct to your store — usually within about two weeks.
