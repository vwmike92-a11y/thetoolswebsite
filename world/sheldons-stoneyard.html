🧱 Sheldon's Stoneyard

Bricks, cement and Sheldon's pride — all in one place

Sheldon's Stoneyard is where all the bricks, cement and rock delivered from Redstone Quarry end up — stacked, sorted, and ready for whatever Twin Falls is building next. Sheldon runs the place with real pride, and it shows.

Between the Heavy Hauler rolling in with fresh loads and the odd friendly showdown with JJ, the Stoneyard is rarely quiet. Somehow, Sheldon always seems to have everything perfectly under control — right up until it isn't.
