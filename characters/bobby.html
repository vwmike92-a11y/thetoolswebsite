Bobby is the mischievous, slightly lazy member of the Bucket trio. Green, always making a face, and found doing the absolute minimum at any given moment. In winter he has a habit of getting frozen solid — which at least gives him a reasonable excuse for doing nothing.

He's not quite as bad as Paz. Nobody is. But Bobby has his moments.
